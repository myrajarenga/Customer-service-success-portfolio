Sample work

How I handle the hard parts.

Sample 1 · Onboarding

The 30-minute setup call

A clear agenda so the customer leaves with a working setup and a next step.

  • Agree what a good month looks like · 5 min
  • Set up the booking page together · 15 min
  • Make a test booking, live · 5 min
  • Agree the next step and follow-up date · 5 min

Sample 2 · Retention

The save message

For a customer who just cancelled. I ask, I listen, and I offer a real fix.

“Hi Sam, I saw you cancelled, and thank you for trying us. Can I ask what was not working? If it was the booking page, I can fix it with you in 15 minutes. If it was price, let’s look at a plan that fits how you actually sell.”

Sample 3 · Payments

The failed-payment follow-up

Three gentle touches. Each makes it easy to fix, and none of them pressure.

  1. Day 0: Friendly note with a one-click fix.
  2. Day 3: Offer to check the invoice together.
  3. Day 7: Short call to explain what pauses and how to keep everything running.

Sample 5 · Expansion

Ask first, suggest second

I only bring up an add-on when the customer’s own numbers show a need.

  1. Ask: “How many calls does your team book each week?”
  2. Ask: “Do different reps sell different offers?”
  3. Suggest a second booking page only if it fits their setup.
